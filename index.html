With Examples & Explanations | For Competitive Exams & Placements

1. PERCENTAGES

Basic Formula

Percentage = (Value / Total) × 100

Successive Percentage Change

Net Change = a + b + (a × b / 100)
Example: A number is increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. Net change = 10 - 10 - (10×10/100) = -1% (Net decrease of 1%).

2. PROFIT & LOSS

Profit% = (SP - CP) / CP × 100  |  Loss% = (CP - SP) / CP × 100
SP = CP × (100 + Profit%) / 100  |  SP = CP × (100 - Loss%) / 100
Example: A pen is bought for ₹200 and sold for ₹250. Profit = ₹50. Profit% = (50/200)×100 = 25%.

3. SIMPLE & COMPOUND INTEREST

SI = (P × R × T) / 100  |  Amount (A) = P + SI
CI Amount = P × (1 + R/100)^T  |  CI = A - P
Example (CI): ₹1000 at 10% for 2 years. A = 1000×(1.10)² = ₹1210. CI = 1210 - 1000 = ₹210.

4. RATIO & PROPORTION

a : b :: c : d → a × d = b × c
Example: Find x if 2:3 :: 4:x. 2x = 12 → x = 6.

5. AVERAGES

Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations)
Average Speed (equal distances) = 2xy / (x + y)
Example: Go at 30 km/h, return at 60 km/h. Avg speed = (2×30×60)/(30+60) = 40 km/h.

6. TIME, SPEED & DISTANCE

Speed = Distance / Time  |  km/h × 5/18 = m/s
Relative Speed (Opposite) = S1 + S2  |  (Same) = S1 - S2
Example: Two trains (200m & 300m) run at 20 m/s and 15 m/s towards each other. Total distance = 500m. Relative speed = 35 m/s. Time = 500/35 ≈ 14.28 sec.

7. TIME & WORK

If A does work in X days → 1 day work = 1/X
A (X days) + B (Y days) together = (X × Y) / (X + Y)
Example: A in 5 days, B in 10 days. Together = (5×10)/(5+10) = 50/15 = 3.33 days.

8. MIXTURES & ALLIGATIONS

(Qty of Cheaper) / (Qty of Dearer) = (Price Dearer - Mean) / (Mean - Price Cheaper)
Example: Rice at ₹50 & ₹70 mixed to get ₹60/kg. Ratio = (70-60) : (60-50) = 10:10 = 1:1.

9. PERMUTATIONS & COMBINATIONS

nPr = n! / (n-r)!  |  nCr = n! / [ r! × (n-r)! ]
Example: Selecting 2 players from 6. 6C2 = (6×5)/(2×1) = 15 ways.

10. PROBABILITY

P(E) = (Favorable Outcomes) / (Total Outcomes)
Example: Rolling a dice, probability of even number. Fav = {2,4,6} = 3. Total = 6. P = 3/6 = 1/2.

11. NUMBER SYSTEM (LCM & HCF)

LCM × HCF = Product of the two numbers
Example: LCM=60, HCF=5, One number=15. Other = (60×5)/15 = 20.

12. ALGEBRAIC IDENTITIES

(a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²  |  a² - b² = (a+b)(a-b)
Example: a+b=5, ab=6. a²+b² = (a+b)² - 2ab = 25 - 12 = 13.

⚡ PRO TIPS FOR YOUR EXAM

  • Read units carefully (km/h vs m/s, rupees vs paise).
  • When stuck on Time & Work, always find "1 day's work" first.
